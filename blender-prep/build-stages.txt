STAGE 2: REUSABLE HUMANOID RIG, NO FULL PERFORMANCE
Armature with root, pelvis, spine/chest, neck/head; left/right upper_arm, forearm, hand, thigh, shin and foot; toe/heel pivots if needed. Distinguish deform bones from controls. Mesh must have an Armature modifier and explicit vertex-group weights; bone parenting loose rigid capsules alone is not an adequate seamless body rig. Normalize weights, inspect shoulder/elbow/hip/knee deformations at representative bends. Foot IK targets, knee pole controls, hand/wrist controls, heel/toe roll and root translation should work without bone stretching. Document actual names, rest axes, units, rest transforms, control meanings and supported limits. Use independent root and pelvis controls so root travel and weight shift can be authored separately.
Face controls: left/right blink or eye opening, gaze, brow raise/tilt, mouth smile and surprise/open. Shape keys or attached curve controls are acceptable if editable and documented. Thumb/finger controls must produce relaxed hand, fist and thumbs-up without replacing hands with inconsistent artwork. Inspect a bent knee/elbow, foot-roll, blink/smile and thumbs-up test pose, return to rest and save. These are rig validation poses only, not an approved animated performance.

STAGE 3: AFTER USER APPROVES LOOK
Author a short walk, stop, turn, blink/smile and thumbs-up using the user's video as the quality benchmark. Contact/down/passing/up poses, opposing arm swing with elbow/wrist follow, pelvis rotation and lateral weight transfer, heel strike and toe-off, planted support feet. Set stride distance from root travel, do not slide feet. Decelerate into a chosen balanced double-support pose, then turn with a step and weight shift rather than pivoting both planted feet. Lead turn with gaze/head and follow with chest/pelvis. Maintain full-body camera and ground for contact review. Keep idle breathing subtle and expressions intentional, not constant mouth/eye jitter. Store walk/stop/turn/gesture as named editable actions and document timing. Do not copy a stiff test cycle and describe it as natural. Same motion intent can retarget only after rest-pose/bone/control mapping and proportion checks, not by blindly copying Euler angles.
